Shop Policies, Downpayments & Warranty

1. Reservation Fee & Downpayment Protection

To prevent no-show appointments and reserve technician bay time, an advance downpayment (₱50.00 standard) is required. The downpayment is 100% credited toward your total final invoice.

2. Customer-Supplied vs. Shop Parts

Customers may bring their own replacement parts (subject to a standard flat ₱50.00 fitment and safety inspection fee). For shop-supplied genuine catalog parts, fitment is completely free with direct itemized retail pricing, backed by our shop warranty.

3. 7-Day Workmanship Warranty

All tune-up, ECU diagnostics, and mechanical repairs are covered by our 7-day workmanship warranty. Defective components supplied by our shop will be replaced at zero labor cost.

4. Vehicle Release & Storage

Bikes marked as Ready for Pick-Up should be retrieved within 3 business days. Digital receipts and itemized payslips are provided upon vehicle clearance.
